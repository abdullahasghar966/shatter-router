`shatter` and `explode` are now unmistakably different. `shatter` is a falling pane: a long visible crack, then heavy glass dropping with little outward speed. `explode` is a blast: no crack, a spider-web fracture, a white flash, a shockwave that lifts the page toward you, a short camera shake, then pieces flung at the camera. New `ShatterEffect.crackDuration`. The `radial` and `rings` patterns now put the requested number of pieces on the page.
