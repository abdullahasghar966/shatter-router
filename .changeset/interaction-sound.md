Opt-in interaction and sound, each a small lazy chunk. `interaction: true` adds a cursor kit that arms over links, press and hold to hit harder, drag across a link to tear the page along the drag, and haptic ticks on touch; requests also take `intensity` and `path`. `sound: true` plays procedural crack, break and falling sounds voiced per motion and material, only after a user gesture and never under reduced motion; `setSound()` toggles it.
