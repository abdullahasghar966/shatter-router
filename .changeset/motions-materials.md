Eleven new motions in four families, each loaded on first use: gravity (`crumble`, `rain`), force (`vortex`, `wind`), order (`blinds`, `domino`, `slide`, `fold`) and matter (`melt`, `ripple`, `glitch`); every one starts exactly at rest and reverses on Back. Seven materials (`glass`, `ice`, `mirror`, `obsidian`, `paper`, `metal`, `holo`) through the `material` option, `transition({ material })`, a composition's `material` or `setMaterial()`; `glassTint` now overrides the material's own tint. Compositions take `timing.easing` (`'linear'`, `'ease-in'`, `'ease-out'`, `'ease-in-out'`). The sides of pieces at rest are no longer drawn, which removes a faint web over the page during Back.
